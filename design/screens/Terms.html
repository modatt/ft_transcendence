Accounts

Anyone can register. Keep one account per person and keep your password private. Under-18s need a parent or guardian's consent before their first job.

Using the machines

Follow the safety rules shown on each machine page and by staff. You need a valid license to run the laser cutter or CNC yourself; otherwise staff run the job for you. Staff may refuse, pause or stop any job that is unsafe.

Your files

You keep ownership of your designs. You confirm you have the right to make them. We don't make weapons, weapon parts, or anything illegal.

Quotes and prices

A quote is an estimate. Your invoice uses the actual material and machine time staff record. If the amount changes by more than 20%, we tell you before the invoice is issued.

Failed jobs

If a job fails because of the design, you pay for the material used. If it fails because of a machine fault, you pay nothing and we can run it again [confirm].

Payment

Pay at the counter by cash, card or bank/CliQ transfer within 7 days of the invoice [confirm]. Unpaid balances over the limit can stop you starting new jobs.

Pickup

Collect finished items within 7 days [confirm]. After 30 days, uncollected items may be recycled.

Liability and changes

We're not responsible for how you use what you make. We can suspend accounts that break these terms. When the terms change, we show the new date here and tell you by email.

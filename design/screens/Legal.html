Who we are

FabLab is a digital fabrication lab in Amman, Jordan. [Operating organization, confirm] For privacy questions, email [privacy contact, confirm].

What we collect

  • Your name, email and mobile number.
  • Optional details: gender and date of birth. You can leave them blank.
  • Student proof, if you ask for student prices.
  • The design files you upload, your jobs, invoices and payments.
  • Security records, such as when you log in.

Why we use it

To make your parts, bill and collect payment, keep machines safe, and send you updates. We also report anonymous totals to our funders. Groups of fewer than 5 people are never shown, so no one can be identified.

Who sees it

Only lab staff and administrators. We never sell your data or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

Design files are deleted 180 days after a job closes. Invoices and payments are kept as tax law requires [period, confirm]. When you delete your account, the rest of your data is made anonymous.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download and delete your data from your profile page, under Jordan's Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023.

Under 18

Accounts for people under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent.

Cookies

We use only a session cookie to keep you logged in and one to remember your language. No tracking or advertising cookies.
